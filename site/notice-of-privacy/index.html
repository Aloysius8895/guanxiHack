Our website uses technical cookies and server logging technologies (logs) for the purpose of remembering the user’s  browsing preferences (such as language), ensuring the security of the infrastructure, and allowing the portal to  operate efficiently and securely.   • Visitor’s IP address. 

  • Date and time of the access request.

In the event that secondary tracking technologies for consumer profiles, advanced analytics, or  behavioral advertising are implemented in the future, an explicit mechanism shall be enabled on the portal so that  you may accept or reject such tools.

If you do not agree with the use of technical cookies, you may prevent their installation through your browser  settings, or delete them subsequently. Please note that, if you limit or block these technologies, some site functions  may not be available.
