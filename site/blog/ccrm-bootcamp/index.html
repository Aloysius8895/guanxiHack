CCRM Bootcamp: Leadership in Action, Every Shift

The first MetriX Bootcamp brought together Directors, Managers, and Safety Coordinators with one clear purpose: to strengthen the implementation of our Critical Risk Management System (CCRM) and reinforce the leadership commitment that defines our company.

More than a training session, this program marked a turning point in how we experience safety at MetriX. Each activity was designed to deepen participants’ understanding of how to identify, assess, and control life-threatening risks, ensuring that every team member returns home safely at the end of the day.

Throughout the event, participants shared insights, analyzed real cases, and engaged in practical exercises that emphasized the importance of performing quality and safety verifications to prevent deaths. The message was clear: every decision matters, every preventive action protects what’s most valuable — our people.

The CCRM is not just a system; it’s a culture that is built through consistent actions. Through visible leadership, effective communication, and disciplined execution, we aim to make safety not a requirement, but a shared value. This program empowers every leader to lead by example, ensuring critical controls are implemented.

The outcome of this first bootcamp goes far beyond training. It represents a solid step toward a stronger, more aware, and more participative safety culture, where everyone takes responsibility for protecting themselves and others.

Today, we reaffirm our commitment: that the CCRM will be lived Every Day, Every Shift, and in Every Activity — because at MetriX, Our Strength is Our People.

“Leadership that Inspires: Safety is not taught — it’s contagious. Example is the best personal protective equipment.”
